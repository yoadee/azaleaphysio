# Creative Direction Brief — Azalea Physiotherapy

## Project

**Name:** Azalea Physiotherapy Website Rebuild  
**Description:** Full rebuild of azaleaphysio.com for a multi-disciplinary wellness clinic in West Vancouver, BC. Two locations, 12 practitioners, 10 services ranging from physiotherapy and sports injury to acupuncture, osteopathy, and elderly care. The current site has missing core pages, placeholder content, and a generic look that undersells the quality of care the clinic provides.  
**Target audience:** Health-conscious, affluent West Vancouver and North Shore residents. Sophisticated enough to distrust healthcare marketing clichés. Decision-makers for their own and their family's care. Often referred by physicians or word of mouth — the site must confirm the referral was right.  
**Business goal:** Convert site visitors into booked appointments. Increase local search visibility for both clinic locations. Establish Azalea as the premium multi-disciplinary clinic on the North Shore.

---

## The Four Axes

**Tone Register: Professional**  
The category is full of performative warmth. Restraint and precision are the differentiator for this audience. Language is measured, trusts the reader, avoids filler phrases and emotional over-reach. Warmth is earned at specific human moments (team bios, testimonials, confirmation messages) — not applied as a blanket tone.

**Aesthetic Philosophy: Editorial Restrained**  
Generous white space. One definitive image per section, not grids. Low colour count deployed with intention. Typography does heavy lifting. Every element earns its place or is cut. Signals confidence, patience, and quality — the same things a premium clinic signals in person.

**Audience Relationship: Companion**  
The patient is the protagonist of their own recovery. Azalea walks alongside them. Not authority (this demographic resists being told what to do), not peer (would undersell the expertise). Companion: present, knowledgeable, deferential to patient agency. Copy says "your recovery" not "our treatment."

**Sensory Ambition: Considered**  
The craft is visible — the Cormorant Garamond headlines, the botanical colour story, the photography — but it serves the content. The reader notices the quality without it being performed at them. Resonant would feel self-indulgent for healthcare. Functional would be too sterile for a luxury brand.

---

## Synthesis

This brief produces website work that feels like a private clinic a trusted friend recommended — quietly excellent, clearly expert, never trying too hard. The typography creates editorial gravity. The palette (dusty rose, sage teal, warm gold on warm white) references the botanical name without being literal about it. White space signals that the clinic is not desperate for your attention. Copy treats the visitor as an intelligent adult making an important decision about their health. The booking CTA is always present but never aggressive. The overall effect: a visitor arrives uncertain and leaves with the sense that this is exactly the kind of place they were looking for.

## Rejection List

- No stock photography. Real clinic, real team, real patients (with permission) only.
- No exclamation marks in headlines or body copy.
- No testimonial walls. Maximum 3–5, presented with care and attribution.
- No hero carousels or image sliders.
- No pop-ups, chat widgets, or exit-intent overlays.
- No bright clinical blues or sterile white-on-white layouts.
- No "caring hands" or "smiling patient" stock imagery clichés.
- No dense text blocks. Copy breathes.
- No lorem ipsum or placeholder content anywhere, ever.
- No generic "we are passionate about your health" copy.
- No modal forms for booking — booking goes to the existing system directly.
