# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Health-conscious, affluent West Vancouver and North Shore residents. Sophisticated enough to distrust healthcare marketing clichés. Decision-makers for their own and their family's care — and for their families'. The majority arrive via physician referral or word of mouth; the site's job is to confirm the referral was right, not to convince a cold visitor. They evaluate credibility, not promises. They will notice craft because they notice it in everything else in their life.

## Product Purpose

Full rebuild of azaleaphysio.com. Multi-disciplinary physiotherapy and wellness clinic: 12 practitioners across physiotherapy, sports injury, acupuncture, osteopathy, kinesiology, occupational therapy, chiropractic, weight loss, yoga therapy, and elderly care. Two West Vancouver locations (16th Street and Ocean Walk). Primary goal: convert site visitors into booked appointments. Secondary: local search visibility for both locations. Tertiary: establish Azalea as the premium multi-disciplinary clinic on the North Shore — above the generic "physio clinic" category.

## Brand Personality

Quietly excellent. Present without performing. The patient is the protagonist of their own recovery; Azalea walks alongside, not ahead. Warmth is earned at human moments (team bios, testimonials, post-booking confirmation) — never broadcast as a brand stance. Never asserts authority. Never performs care.

Three words: **Measured. Grounded. Exact.**

## Anti-references

- Any generic physiotherapy or chiropractic clinic site (clinical blue palette, smiling-patient stock, "Your health is our priority")
- Wellness/spa aesthetics: essential oils, candlelight, softness as a brand value
- Corporate healthcare: hierarchical, procedural, cold
- Performative warmth: "We are passionate about your health" copy
- SaaS/tech aesthetics: anything that reads as a tool or product, not a place
- Busy, dense layouts — the site should never feel like it's trying too hard to inform

## Design Principles

1. **Patient as protagonist.** Every headline, layout choice, and CTA is oriented around the patient's recovery — not the clinic's credentials. "Your recovery" not "our treatment."
2. **Earned authority.** Trust is built through demonstrated depth: team bios with real credentials, specific testimonials, granular service descriptions. Never claimed through adjectives.
3. **Restraint as signal.** The target audience responds to what's left out. White space, short copy, one definitive image per section — all communicate that the clinic is confident, not anxious.
4. **Confirm the referral.** Most visitors arrive recommended. The site's single job at that moment: make them feel the referral was right. Not "discover Azalea" — validate the decision they've already half-made.
5. **Craft is the brand.** This audience notices execution. Typography, spacing, image quality, and copy precision are not decoration — they ARE the brand promise made tangible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Keyboard navigable throughout. No motion-gated content; all animations must degrade gracefully under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Several team members are Persian-speaking; a large portion of the North Shore market is Persian-Canadian — Farsi language version is a future consideration worth designing for (RTL-ready component structure recommended). Booking flow must be operable without a mouse.
