## PAGE: /insurance

**Page H1:**
_Insurance & Direct Billing — West Vancouver_

**Intro paragraph:**
Yes — Azalea direct-bills most insurers, so you don't pay the covered portion upfront. We bill ICBC, WorkSafeBC and the major extended health plans directly, settle with them on your behalf, and you cover only the balance your plan doesn't, if any. Here's how it works for each.

---

### H2: ICBC Claims

ICBC covers physiotherapy for injuries from a motor vehicle accident, and you can start treatment without an adjuster's pre-approval. Under BC's Enhanced Care, you're pre-authorized for a set number of physiotherapy visits in the weeks after your accident, billed directly to ICBC.

**How to start an ICBC claim at Azalea:**
1. Report your accident to ICBC and get your claim number.
2. Call either clinic and tell us you have an ICBC claim.
3. Book your first assessment — no referral or lawyer needed to begin.

**What to bring to your first ICBC visit:**
- Your ICBC claim number
- Your date of accident
- A piece of photo ID
- Your BC Services Card (CareCard)

---

### H2: WorkSafeBC Claims

WorkSafeBC covers physiotherapy for injuries that happened at work, and we bill them directly. Once your claim is open, we coordinate the treatment plan and reporting with WorkSafeBC, including the documentation for a managed return to work.

**How to start a WorkSafeBC claim at Azalea:**
1. Report your injury to your employer and to WorkSafeBC, and get your claim number.
2. Call either clinic and tell us it's a WorkSafeBC claim.
3. Book your assessment — we handle the billing and reporting from there.

**What to bring:**
- Your WorkSafeBC claim number
- Your date of injury
- A piece of photo ID

---

### H2: Extended Health Plans

We direct-bill most major extended health insurers, including Pacific Blue Cross, Sun Life, Manulife, Canada Life, Green Shield and Desjardins. In practice, that means we submit the claim at the desk and you pay only what your plan doesn't cover — not the full fee followed by a wait for reimbursement.

Coverage varies by policy: some plans need a doctor's referral to reimburse, and most cap the number of visits or dollar amount per year. We'll tell you what your plan returns before you commit to a course of treatment. If we can't direct-bill your specific insurer, we give you a receipt formatted for an easy claim.

---

### H2: Pricing

We publish our fees because you shouldn't have to call to find out. An initial physiotherapy assessment runs roughly $110–$135, and follow-up visits roughly $90–$110, depending on length and practitioner. Most extended health plans reimburse a significant share of this, and where we direct-bill, you pay only the balance at the desk. ICBC and WorkSafeBC visits are typically covered in full, billed directly, with nothing for you to pay upfront.

---

### Insurance FAQ

**Q: Do I pay anything upfront if you direct-bill my insurance?**
A: Usually not for the covered portion. We bill your insurer directly and you pay only the balance your plan doesn't cover — often nothing for ICBC and WorkSafeBC claims, and a small co-pay for many extended health plans.

**Q: What if you can't direct-bill my specific insurer?**
A: You pay at the visit and we give you a detailed receipt formatted for reimbursement. You submit it to your insurer and they pay you back according to your policy.

**Q: Does my extended health plan need a doctor's referral?**
A: Sometimes. BC doesn't require a referral to receive physiotherapy, but some extended health policies require one to reimburse you. Check your plan booklet, or call your insurer's member line, before your first visit.

## PAGE: /what-to-expect

**Page H1:**
_Your First Visit_

**Intro paragraph:**
If you've never been to a physiotherapist — or your last one wasn't much help — here's exactly what happens, start to finish. There's nothing to prepare for and no test to pass. Just come as you are.

**The steps:**

1. **Book.** Reserve online or by phone. No referral is needed, and most new patients are seen within the same week.

2. **Check in.** Arrive ten minutes early to fill in a short intake form. Bring your insurance details so we can set up direct billing before you're seen.

3. **Tell us the story.** Your practitioner asks how the injury happened, what makes it better or worse, and what you're hoping to get back to.

4. **Get assessed.** They examine how the area moves and where it hurts, to find the cause rather than just the sore spot. Wear or bring clothing you can move in.

5. **Hear the plan in plain language.** Your practitioner explains what they found, what's likely going on, and how many visits it should take.

6. **Start treatment the same day.** Most first visits include hands-on treatment and a few exercises to begin at home — you don't leave with only a diagnosis.

7. **Book your follow-up.** We schedule the next visit and set up any direct billing before you go.

**What to bring:**
- A piece of photo ID
- Your insurance or extended health details
- Your ICBC or WorkSafeBC claim number, if you have one
- Clothing you can move comfortably in
- Any relevant scans, reports or surgical notes, if you have them

**Final note / CTA:**
First visits run about 45 to 60 minutes. When you're ready, book online or call the clinic closest to you — 16th Street at (604) 281-3345, or Ocean Walk at (604) 281-3122.
